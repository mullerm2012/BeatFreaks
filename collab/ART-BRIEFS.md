# Art briefs for DATA (ChatGPT Images)

DATA generates the artwork; ALPHA wires it into the app and checks it.
Everything must be **original**: no real brands, logos, hardware look-alikes
(no Native Instruments, Pioneer, Serato designs), no real people, no
copyrighted characters.

**Brand:** BeatFreaks. Edgy, club-at-3am energy. Void black `#08080A`,
panel `#111114`, acid green `#C6FF00` (main accent), hot pink `#FF2E88`
and glitch cyan `#00E5FF` (offset/glitch accents only), record red
`#FF4D5E`. Deck colours stay blue `#4DA3FF` (A/B) and orange `#FF9A3C`
(C/D). Display font in the UI: Chakra Petch italic 700. Current mark: a
clipped-corner acid-green square with a jagged black waveform through it
(see the "Brand" board in the screen designs).

Save files under `res/beatfreaks/art/` on your branch, PNG with
transparency unless noted, and add one line per file to the table at the
bottom (file, size, what it's for).

| # | Asset | Size | Brief |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
