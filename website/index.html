Three ways to play

Switch at any time. The same songs, decks and recordings follow you between modes.

DJ

Four decks, sync, key lock, loops, hot cues and effects, built for the Traktor Kontrol S4 MK3 and F1 and 160+ other controllers.

Arrange

An ACID-style timeline: a song or loop on each track, tempo-matched, with automation lanes for every control.

Hybrid

Play the decks live while the arranger records. Punch in, touch or latch any lane, then go back and tidy the take.

Live moves become lanes

Pitch, EQ, filter, FX and platter moves are recorded as automation on the timeline. Edit one fader move without redoing the mix.

AI songs for the floor

When the floor goes quiet, BeatFreaks writes a request in your key and tempo for the free, offline ACE-Step model, or a ready-to-paste Suno prompt. New downloads land in your queue.

In development

Played list and mix cover

Every set produces a timestamped tracklist, YouTube chapters, a CUE sheet that splits the recording, and a cover for the mix.

Every controller, every button

BeatFreaks ships 164 ready-made controller mappings. For gear that isn't covered yet (first up: Pioneer DDJ-WeGO4 and Numark 4TRAK), a capture tool records your controller and we build a full mapping from it. A button editor lets you put any BeatFreaks action on any button, with no code.

Questions

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once. Version 1 updates are included. If a future version 2 is a big step up, upgrades will also be a one-time price, and version 1 keeps working.

Can I use it on my phone?

First comes the phone remote: your phone controls BeatFreaks on your PC over Wi-Fi. A full BeatFreaks for tablets and phones is planned later. Mixxx, which BeatFreaks is built on, already has experimental mobile builds.

Can I use Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, Beatport or SoundCloud?

Not yet. These services only allow DJ apps they have approved, and we are applying. Today BeatFreaks plays the music files you own, from your PC or your cloud folder.

Does it work with my Traktor S4 MK3 and F1?

Yes. Both have built-in mappings, and BeatFreaks adds its own features to their spare buttons.

Why is it paid if it is open source?

The GPL licence lets anyone sell copies and requires that buyers can get the source code. BeatFreaks follows that. What you pay for is the official signed installer, updates, support and the online services.

Will my Mac work?

Windows comes first. A Mac version is possible later because the engine is cross-platform.
