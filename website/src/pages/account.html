Download after you buy

  1. Open My downloads.

    Click "Sign in to My downloads" and type the email address you used to buy BeatFreaks.

  2. Check your email.

    You get a secure sign-in link. Open it in the same browser. It keeps you signed in until you sign out.

  3. Download the installer.

    Under your BeatFreaks order, click the BeatFreaks for Windows download. New versions appear in the same place.

  4. Copy your licence key.

    It is shown on the same page and in your receipt email. BeatFreaks asks for it the first time it starts.

  5. Install.

    Run the installer. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The installation guide walks through every screen.

Computers and devices

Two computers per purchase

Activate BeatFreaks on up to 2 of your computers. Getting a new PC? Remove the old one under your licence in My downloads, then activate the new one.

Online extras on 3 devices

The phone remote, AI song cloud and cloud folder work on up to 3 of your devices with your BeatFreaks account, as each one launches.

Lost the email?

Sign in to My downloads with your purchase email to see your order again. Still stuck? Contact support with your order number.

Changed your email?

Contact support with your order number and we will move the order to your new address.
