Help

Questions

Can't find your answer? The user manual covers every screen step by step.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once and every version 1 update is included. If a future version 2 is a big step up, the upgrade will also be a one-time price, and version 1 keeps working.

How do I download after buying?

Go to My downloads, type the email you bought with, and open the sign-in link we email you. Your installer and licence key are there.

How many computers can I use?

Two per purchase. Moving to a new PC? Remove the old one in My downloads and activate the new one.

Does it work with my Traktor S4 MK3 and F1?

Yes. Both have built-in mappings.

Does it work with the Pioneer DDJ-WeGO4 or Numark 4TRAK?

Not yet. They are next on the list.

Can I use it on my phone?

A phone remote that controls BeatFreaks on your PC is planned first. A full version for phones and tablets may follow later.

Can I use Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, Beatport or SoundCloud?

Not yet. These services only allow DJ apps they have approved, and we are applying. BeatFreaks plays the music files you own.

Is AI music included?

BeatFreaks is being built to request songs from the free ACE-Step model on your own PC, or to prepare a prompt for Suno. You decide whether an AI song plays. Check the rights for any song before you play it in public or publish a mix.

Why do you charge if it is open source?

The GPL licence lets anyone sell copies and requires that buyers can get the source code. BeatFreaks follows that. You pay for the official signed installer, updates, support and the online services.

Will there be a Mac version?

Windows comes first. A Mac version is possible later because the engine runs on Mac too.

How do I get a refund?

Within 14 days of purchase. See the refund policy.
