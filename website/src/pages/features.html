BeatFreaks extras

In development

Smart Next

Energy score for every song, then a ranked "play next" list by Camelot key, tempo with half/double time, energy direction (build, hold, cool down), crowd response and genre.

In development

Live moves become lanes

Pitch, EQ, filter, FX and platter moves recorded as automation in Touch, Latch or punch-in modes. Edit one move without redoing the mix.

In development

Arrange mode

An ACID-style timeline with tempo-matched tracks, saved as a BeatFreaks project file.

In development

AI songs for the floor

Song requests for the free, offline ACE-Step 1.5 model with DJ-friendly 32-bar intros and outros, a Suno prompt builder, and a Song Inbox that queues new downloads. You approve every AI song before it plays.

In development

Played list and mix cover

Tracklist with times, keys and BPM; YouTube chapters; CUE sheet; CSV for spreadsheets; a mix cover with your title and date.

In development

Hardware test screen

Every button and knob lights up on screen when you touch it, with a report you can send us.

Coming later

Autopilot

Finds phrases and breaks, picks a transition (long blend, filter rise, loop roll, double drop, echo out, spinback) and mixes on the bar. Every move it makes is recorded as lanes you can edit.

Coming later

Crowd cam

A webcam measures how much the floor moves and whether it moves on the beat. Motion only: no faces, nothing saved, off until you switch it on.

Coming later

Phone remote

Scan a QR code and your phone controls BeatFreaks over Wi-Fi. Includes crowd song requests that you approve.

Coming later

Stream Deck and OBS

Elgato Stream Deck keys and OBS scene switching from the decks.

Coming later

Cloud folder

Save recordings, crates and projects to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox. Checks your songs are offline before a gig.

Coming later

Streaming services

Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, Beatport and SoundCloud only allow approved DJ apps. We are applying; until then BeatFreaks plays music files you own.
