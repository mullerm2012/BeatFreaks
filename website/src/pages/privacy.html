Privacy

The app

BeatFreaks runs on your computer. Your music, library, recordings and settings stay on your computer unless you choose a cloud folder.

Logs are only sent when you choose to send them from the Help menu. They contain technical details such as your controller model, sound card and error messages.

The crowd cam, when it ships, measures motion only. It does not recognise faces, and frames are never saved or uploaded. It is off until you switch it on.

Purchases

Our payment partner processes your name, email, address and payment details to complete your order and send receipts. We receive your name, email and order details to provide downloads and support.

Website

This website does not use advertising trackers.
